# DCM Shriram Industries' demerger, filled in for you

If you held **DCM Shriram Industries** before its December 2025 demerger,
this update completes the picture for you.

On the next run you'll see **two new rows** appear — DCM Shriram Fine
Chemicals and DCM Shriram International — one share of each for every DCM
Shriram Industries share you held on the record date (26-12-2025). Your
original cost is split between the three companies the way the company
told shareholders to split it: **42.66%** stays with DCM Shriram
Industries, **25.22%** goes to Fine Chemicals and **32.12%** to
International. Each new row keeps your original buy date, so the taxman's
long-term clock keeps running from when you actually bought.

**Your share count in DCM Shriram Industries does not change — and that's
correct.** A demerger doesn't take shares away from you; it hands you
shares in the spun-off companies as well. (A *split* or *bonus* is the
one that changes your count, and "Qty today" has always handled those.)

Nothing to type, and nothing you already typed is touched: the new rows
are added for you, and your own Quantity and Avg. cost cells are never
rewritten. Run the update once with this version and the three rows will
be there.

Mergers and demergers ship with the app like this because no exchange
publishes them in a form we can read reliably. If one you own is missing,
tell us and it goes into the next release — or add it yourself as a
Manual row on the Corporate_Actions tab.
