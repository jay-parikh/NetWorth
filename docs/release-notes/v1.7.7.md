# Corporate actions, without waiting for a new version

Splits and bonuses have always looked after themselves — the exchanges
publish them, so the updater just picks them up.

**Mergers and demergers are different.** Nobody publishes them in a form a
program can read: the share ratio and, more importantly, the split of your
original cost between the old company and the new one, exist only inside
the company's own filing to the exchanges. Someone has to read that filing
and write the numbers down. That's what the project does for you.

Until now those numbers only reached you inside a **new version of the
app** — so if your stock demerged, you waited for a release.

**From this version, they come to you.** Every time you run *Update
Portfolio*, it quietly checks the project for newly published mergers and
demergers and uses them straight away. A demerger added today shows up on
your very next update: the new company appears as its own row, with your
original buy date and your cost split the way the company told
shareholders to split it. **Nothing for you to type, nothing to download.**

The list that ships inside the app is still there and still used, so
everything works exactly as before if your computer is offline or behind a
company firewall. A download that looks wrong in any way — incomplete, or
missing events the app already knows about — is ignored rather than
trusted, and your update carries on regardless. As always, anything you
typed on the Corporate_Actions tab yourself still wins.

Also in this release: the **Hindustan Unilever ice-cream demerger**
(Kwality Wall's (India), record date 05-12-2025, one share each, cost split
98.09% / 1.91%) is now included.

*If you would rather the app never fetched this list, run it with
`--no-curated-fetch`.*
