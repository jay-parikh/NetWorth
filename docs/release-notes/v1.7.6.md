# Your family list stays your family list

If your Dashboard ever grew tabs called **TOTAL**, **Dividends FY 2026‑27**
or **Allocation by asset class**, this release removes them and explains
what happened.

Those are headings on the Dashboard, not people. The updater reads your
family's names from the block of rows under *Person* — and if the empty
rows in that block get deleted (a perfectly reasonable tidy‑up, and the
guide does say you may delete rows freely), everything below slides up into
that block. The word **TOTAL** lands where a name should be, and the
updater dutifully treated it as a family member: its own tab, its own
column, its own row in the grid.

**What's fixed.** The updater now knows its own headings and stops reading
names when it reaches one. Your real family list is read correctly whether
those rows were deleted or not.

**What happens to a file that already has them.** Nothing for you to do.
The next update quietly drops them, tells you in one line that it did, and
rebuilds the Dashboard properly — the invented tabs disappear. Your own
tabs, your holdings and every number you typed are untouched.

Everything else in this release is unchanged.
